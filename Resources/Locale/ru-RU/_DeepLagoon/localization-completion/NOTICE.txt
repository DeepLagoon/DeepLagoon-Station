Russian prototype localization sources

Translations were matched by prototype ID and original text against the local
arcane-master, crystall-edge-master and sector-frontier-14-master source archives.
Only resource text was reused. Additional translations and adaptations were made
for DeepLagoon. Existing Russian localization was retained where applicable.
