# Bitrunning map import

The importer reproduces the 26 WhiteMoon domain grids, 14 safehouse grids and two mining shelter templates from the source commit recorded in map_manifest.json. It requires Python 3.11 or newer, Pillow and PyYAML. Use a Git checkout that contains the source commit. The importer reads DMM, DM and DMI blobs from Git.

    py -3 Tools/bitrunning/import_maps.py C:\old_disk\repos\ss13\WhiteMoon-Station
    py -3 Tools/bitrunning/import_maps.py C:\old_disk\repos\ss13\WhiteMoon-Station --verify

Every generated map records its source path, digest and dimensions. The manifest lists the encountered source atom paths and their target tiles, entities, decals or map structures. An empty target list identifies a source atom without a native mapping. An unavailable prototype is recorded by name. The source maps retain BYOND-specific area metadata and atom fields for further work.

Public prototype IDs use the source DM type, with descriptive state or placement suffixes where needed. They do not include import counters or content hashes. Real source model numbers (such as a weapon model) remain meaningful. Digests are confined to asset filenames and provenance metadata. The importer fails if it cannot derive an unambiguous source name. Regression checks: `py -3 -m unittest discover -s Tools/bitrunning/tests -v`.

The importer joins the domains to their authored safehouses using the modular anchor and connector. For a safehouse with no connector the anchor goes at the lower-left cell. Transparent template cells retain their original floor. The exported safehouse files are standalone grids for mapping and verification. Virtual grids keep their disconnected floor islands together, with grid splitting disabled.

Unanchored items mapped on a closet or crate tile are serialized in its entity storage. Authored `PopulateContents` executes through native entity tables, preserving counts, probabilities and mutually exclusive choices. Power cables use the native sprites required by the cable visualizer. APC panels use the WhiteMoon wall offset, and wide airlocks use directional frames and a fixture that spans both tiles.

The grids preserve the main routes and scenario locations using native DeepLagoon assets. Specific adaptations include a linked gateway for the bluespace peel in Clown Planet, destructible resin in Xeno Nest, and a catwalk to the Blood Drunk Miner fight. The glacier uses passable stair tiles and hazardous cliff edges in the single-level grid. Ancient Milsim retains its yellow and blue keycard doors. Psyker Zombies has native firearm supply crates at its mystery box locations, and Pipedream uses native straight and bent disposal pipes. Dark areas and scenario progression are configured by the bitrunning runtime.

## WhiteMoon textures

The importer resolves each placed object's DMM sprite overrides and its inherited WhiteMoon DM icon properties in project include order. It uses the runtime side of `MAP_SWITCH`. It exports the original frames, directional states and delays from DMI into RSI. Generated entities inherit the port's existing prototypes and localization. Floor variants inherit their native tile's physical properties. The generated map appearances are local to Bitrunning.

The map art pack restores visible objects omitted in the initial conversion, including embedded and standalone copies of safehouses. Objects without native gameplay equivalents appear as scenery or can be picked up as ordinary items. This fallback is incomplete gameplay, not a faithful port. An imported garment needs its original worn appearance and slot; a weapon needs its source firing, ammunition and reload behavior. A matching ground sprite alone does not establish compatibility.

Clothing imports use the inherited DM `worn_icon` and `worn_icon_state`, including the default atlases selected by WhiteMoon's human inventory renderer. Authored GAGS icon layers and colors generate the worn layers, including digitigrade configurations and available `_d` states. These are exported as explicit `-digitigrade` clothing visuals. Any unsupported worn art is reported rather than counted as a working garment. Native atmospheric connection visualizers retain native pipe art until their source connection states are adapted. Non-dense WhiteMoon railing corners are non-colliding in the imported grids. Large tree art is centered relative to BYOND's lower-left icon anchor so its trunk stays on its physical cell.

## Functional import

`source_model.py` reads inherited properties and initialization procedures in project include order. `gameplay_import.py` adapts weapons, ammunition, modkits, clothing properties, source outfits, ghost roles, NPC attacks and storage contents into `WhiteMoonGameplay.yml`. It is an explicit adapter, not a general DM interpreter. Unsupported storage control flow fails the import. Missing item adapters are listed in `map_manifest.json` under `functionalImport.unresolvedItems`; they must not be treated as completed ports. Boss phase AI remains a separate, incomplete adapter. Native aliases can also differ from source behavior, especially chemistry, special melee abilities, tools and machines.

Generated source entities keep their own localization across visual variants. `translations_ru.tsv`, `descriptions_ru.tsv` and the multiline `descriptions_ru.json` supply authored Russian text; ammunition caliber suffixes are translated separately. Remaining names and descriptions are listed in the map manifest. Generated files do not make untranslated source text disappear from the audit. Historic entity IDs have migrations in `Resources/migration.yml` for saved maps.

Runtime modkits occupy a container on the accelerator, consume the source capacity, affect actual projectiles and recharge, and can be removed. Source magazines have explicit caliber and magazine-family compatibility; chambered rounds consume starting magazine capacity. Energy modes switch actual projectiles and battery cost. Domain actors use temporary ghost-role minds and the source NPC friendship. The island objective counts participant deaths. Glitch events mutate unoccupied domain NPCs and use the source threat probability, with a manual station-event entry point and domain cleanup.

## Special callback coverage

`special_items.py` supplies explicit adapters for all 18 of the original special-item paths. This counts explicit adapters, not full source parity. The mining voucher offers all eight source reward sets, with real populated bags, resonator fields, minebot accelerator and ore collection, deployable source shelter maps and hunter boxing mitts. The elite MOD retains Syndicate access, source cell/capacity, four deployed parts, nine installed and three inbuilt modules, and normal, sealed and digitigrade visuals. Elite boots inherit plain footwear rather than native standard MOD boots, so sealing does not grant magboots or anti-slip protection without their source modules.

Beacon colors use all twelve source colors and their original states/light values; placement rejects another beacon on the same tile. Retrieval uses an interruptible 1.5-second do-after and can merge into a held stack. The dish drive derives its square suction range and power requirement from installed machine parts, ticks every two seconds, and rejects loaded plates and drinks during automatic collection. Source dish ancestry is recorded explicitly instead of inferred from displayed names. The silver telescopic baton uses 30-point melee armor penetration for its stamina strike. The command whip applies the source movement buff for seven seconds: at the pinned revision its declared action-speed modifier is never assigned to the status effect (`action_datum` stays null), so an additional action-speed buff would change source behavior.

The implemented callbacks cover flower reagent spraying, the ClF3 grenade, single-item evidence storage, disco deployment, beacon placement, wearable waistcoat art, baton extension and stun, command-whip movement buff, rideable hoverboard deployment, timed mirage creation, rechargeable laser pointer, charged carved runes, the dish-drive board and machine, abductor baton modes, 24 long balloons with source color combinations, and all six suit-voucher choices at the mining terminal. Items spawned directly use the same adapters as map placements.

Armor coverage is applied to the struck body region, and untargeted protection averages the six source body regions. Native hands, feet and groin remain separately targetable. Armor category overrides and penetration reach actual limb damage as well as the main health pool. Source attacks subtract percentage points of clothing protection; they do not use the native coefficient exponent or pierce innate megafauna damage susceptibility. Native armor without source coverage retains its normal coefficient combination.

Elite modules run native storage limits, baton resistance, EMP shielding, magnetic weapon return, jetpack, charged jump jet, flashlight, hat mounting, restraint assistance, welding protection and night vision. The hearing module records source immunity, but this fork has no ear injury/deafness backend; it cannot demonstrate a hearing-damage effect until that backend exists. Source wound modifiers, precise jetpack stabilization controls and flashlight customization are not yet equivalent.

Remaining source branches must stay visible when using the manifest's resolved count:

- Waistcoat attachment to a uniform; evidence-bag filled overlays.
- Hoverboard riding stunts and special terrain interactions.
- Mirage projectile collisions and NPC reactions beyond its copied appearance and lifetime.
- Pointer diode/crystal upgrades, camera EMP and species/robot reactions.
- Rune confusion/dizziness, embedding and null-rod interactions.
- Dish-drive power accounting uses native continuous APC load; WhiteMoon charges per transmitted trash item.
- Abductor probe eligibility/gland checks and robot-specific effects.
- Long balloons' monkey-projectile popping and coin behavior.

## Megafauna callback coverage

The boss handlers are separate from generated equipment. The virtual-domain health rule is `clamp(source_max_health * 0.5, 600, 1300)`. Colossus and wendigo use 1250 HP; the blood-drunk miner uses 600. Their patterns run actual projectiles with source damage, speed, timing and health gates. Kinetic subtypes retain mining behavior rather than inheriting pistol behavior.

`BitrunColossusSystem` implements spiral, random, shotgun and alternating volleys, the double spiral below one third HP, and the single-use finale at one tenth HP. `BitrunMinerSystem` implements the four-tile reachable-ground dash, accelerator and transforming saw damage/rate. `BitrunWendigoSystem` implements ground-slam rings, movement stomps, teleport/shotgun and alternating, spiral and curved-wave attacks, including half-health rage and movement locking during stationary sequences.

These handlers still need the source's remaining auxiliary behavior: colossus telegraph presentation, bolt environmental destruction and corpse gutting, its martial-art/gold-golem reaction; miner cleave, devouring, damage interruption and dash presentation; wendigo scream dizziness and presentation. Ash drake, Bubblegum and hierophant now each use 1250 HP, the source virtual-domain health rule, and dedicated autonomous phase handlers instead of generic guns or goliath attacks. Drake executes delayed three-line fire, meteor showers, swoop invulnerability and landing damage, mass fire, and the three-round half-health dodge arena with safe cells and escape healing. Bubblegum leaves cleanable blood, warps onto the outer blood-pool ring, gains timed projectile immunity, performs delayed smack/grab attacks, real multi-step triple charges, hallucination charges and surrounding attacks. Hierophant executes delayed melee-armored heat blasts, blink blasts, expanding bursts, moving chasers and rage-dependent major sequences. Its source area restrictions are retained when authored. The pinned virtual-domain map uses virtual areas rather than the lavaland ruin area, so it keeps the source initial enclosure and periodic arena behavior. Death bursts continue after the boss entity disappears.

Phase handlers still require manual encounter playtesting. Remaining auxiliary parity includes source audiovisual presentation and mecha-specific branches, Bubblegum's chewing-gum reward and station-level devour rules, native lava ignition/catwalk protection differences, and the earlier bosses' auxiliary branches above. Do not infer full parity from sprites, a passed phase test or the absence of unresolved item paths.

All source megafauna tolerate vacuum and unlimited body temperature, reject stamina damage, and do not inherit the humanoid 400-blunt gib threshold. Boss detection uses the megafauna component, including imported appearance variants; a boss domain with no registered boss fails to launch instead of giving a free cache. Boss death records objective completion and the cache location synchronously. Bosses with source `del_on_death = TRUE` are removed; drake and wendigo retain corpses. A completed cache must not be lost or duplicated when the boss disappears.

Integration tests exercise source equipment callbacks, powered dish suction/disposal, voucher single redemption, balloon contents/combinations, boss phase gates and actual shots, autonomous AI target acquisition, and the full colossus domain through death, cache upload, single payout and return to the real body. These checks do not replace manual client playtesting or establish that the remaining source branches work.

The [art manifest](../../Resources/Textures/_DeepLagoon/Bitrunning/WhiteMoon/art_manifest.json) lists the source, state and target of each export. Animated floor textures use native anchored sprites that disappear when their physical tile changes. Native interactions continue to drive the open, closed, damaged and powered states. Where WhiteMoon builds an appearance from an outfit, picks random art in DM or changes color and overlays at runtime, the generated assets reuse the available source states with DeepLagoon's appearance logic. Runtime equipment, avatar and encounter assets from the separate sprite importer have their own [manifest](../../Resources/Textures/_DeepLagoon/Bitrunning/source_states.json).
